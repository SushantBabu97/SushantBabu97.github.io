## Hamro Gifts: Gift E-commerce Platform

An e-commerce platform enabling people abroad to send gifts to recipients in Nepal with a smooth ordering and delivery experience.

### Purpose

- Enable Nepali diaspora to send gifts to Nepal
- Provide seamless gift selection and delivery
- Support local gift vendors and logistics

### Tech Stack

- **Backend**: Node.js
- **Search**: Elasticsearch for efficient product discovery
- **Database**: MongoDB for flexible product catalog
- **Architecture**: Plugin-based architecture for extensibility

### Key Features

- Product search and discovery with Elasticsearch
- Gift customization options
- Multiple payment methods
- Tracking and delivery management
- Plugin architecture for vendor integrations
