## Hamro Patro News: Recommendation System

Redesigned and re-engineered the news recommendation system for Hamro Patro, one of the platform's most-used services, incorporating advanced vector embeddings and semantic search.

### Impact

- Powers one of the most-used services on Hamro Patro
- Millions of users receive personalized news recommendations
- Significantly improved content discovery and user engagement
